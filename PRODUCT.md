# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Patients who want to understand coloproctology care and book a visit with Dra. Dayara Salomão in Campo Grande. Public copy is for that reader.

## Product Purpose

The public website explains who the doctor is, which conditions and treatments she describes, and how to book. Success is a visitor who finds the right page and can reach the clinic.

## Positioning

A coloproctologist practicing at Instituto do Aparelho Digestivo in Campo Grande (MS). The site is the doctor's own pages, not a clinic-wide directory and not a diagnosis tool.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from search, shared links, and the clinic. Booking happens through the shared WhatsApp message and the published phone number. The same URLs also answer `Accept: text/markdown` for agents.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Brazilian Portuguese, patient-friendly and factual. No diagnosis, guaranteed outcomes, or aggressive marketing language.
- Public routes cover the homepage, profile, treatments, blog, Campo Grande location, FAQ, and privacy policy.
- Campo Grande is the only practice location. Curitiba remains only where professional history requires it.
- Contact details, credentials, and medical facts must stay aligned with `src/constants.ts` and the pages that already publish them.
- Unknown URLs must recover the visitor without inventing a destination.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Dra. Dayara Salomão. The voice is calm, direct, and clinical. New pages use the existing design system; they do not introduce another visual identity.

## Evidence on Hand

Published pages, treatment and blog content, credentials, Campo Grande address, WhatsApp, and phone. Do not invent testimonials, prices, outcomes, or a Doctoralia profile until a confirmed Campo Grande URL exists.

## Product Principles

- Say what the page is for, then offer a real next step.
- Publish only what the doctor and the clinic already stand behind.
- Keep the patient path and the Markdown representation on the same facts.
- Prefer the existing components and tokens when adding a page.
- A dead end should name the problem and show the way back.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Semantic headings, meaningful alt text, keyboard access, visible focus, and readable contrast. No product-specific standard beyond those basics was set.

# 404

Scope: unmatched URLs and `notFound()` for the public site.
Visitor mode: Operate. The visitor needs to leave a dead address and reach a real page or the clinic.

Audience: a patient who followed a bad link.
Job: understand the address does not exist, then choose Início, Tratamentos, Blog, or WhatsApp.
Constraints: Brazilian Portuguese; existing design system; no humor, no giant error-code poster, no new medical claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A missing address is a calm recovery. The page names the problem in one sentence and puts four real destinations in reach. It refuses the category poster of a giant 404.

OWN-WORLD: Cream page, teal Cinzel title, Montserrat body, copper primary button, teal secondary button, beige-edged LinkCards. The subpage header and footer stay as they are on the other routes.

STORY: The visitor learns the address is not on the site, returns home or opens WhatsApp from the first actions, and can also open Tratamentos or the Blog.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Subpage header, then a left-aligned h1 "Página não encontrada", the explanation, and two buttons — "Ir para o início" in copper and "Falar no WhatsApp" in teal. Below, the heading "Para onde seguir" and two destination cards, Tratamentos and Blog. No eyebrow and no display numeral.

FORM: Established subpage recovery. The request pinned the existing design system and the four destinations, so that composition stays. seed:4178de53375a91f9

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

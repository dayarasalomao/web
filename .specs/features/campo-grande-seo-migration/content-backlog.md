## Suggested Publishing Sequence

Avoid publishing all posts in one PR. A gradual sequence gives each cluster room to be indexed and reduces near-duplicate risk.

1. Symptom-intent foundation:
   - Sangramento anal ou dor
   - Dor ao evacuar
   - Lesões, verrugas ou nódulos na região anal
2. High-volume condition foundation:
   - Doença hemorroidária
   - Fissura anal
   - HPV anal
   - Cisto pilonidal
3. New treatment-page support:
   - Prurido anal
   - Constipação intestinal
   - Diarreia crônica
   - Síndrome do intestino irritável
4. Specialty-depth support:
   - Fístula anal
   - Doenças inflamatórias intestinais
   - Hidradenite supurativa
5. Campo Grande local-intent variants:
   - Only after active location facts are confirmed.
   - Avoid publishing Campo Grande availability claims before address, clinic, phone, map, and schedule are confirmed.

## Writing Rules For Future Posts

- Use Brazilian Portuguese.
- Keep the tone educational, sober, and patient-friendly.
- Avoid guarantees, "melhor", "cura garantida", fear-based language, or exaggerated claims.
- Do not claim Campo Grande availability until confirmed.
- Each post needs a distinct search intent, not just a rephrased treatment page.
- End with doctor signature and educational disclaimer, following the existing blog pattern.
- Link from symptom posts to condition posts, from condition posts to treatment pages, and from treatment pages back to relevant posts when available.

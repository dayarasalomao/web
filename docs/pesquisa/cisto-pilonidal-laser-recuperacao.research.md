# cisto-pilonidal-laser-recuperacao — scoped evidence notes

Accessed 2026-11-26. Origin: draft supplied by Dra. Dayara (Nov/2026), checked against sources.
Scope: **recovery after laser (SiLaC) for pilonidal disease** — sitting, work, training,
healing, recurrence. Not technique comparison (`cisto-pilonidal-cirurgia-convencional-x-laser`),
not EPSiT procedure (`cisto-pilonidal-laser-epsit`). Notes only.

## Verified

- [COHORT] Tyrväinen 2024, Lasers Med Sci 39:52 (Kuopio, Finland; full text read): sitting
  prohibition (weeks) laser 0.0 / direct closure 2.5 / flap 3.2; sick leave (days) 6.2 / 27.4 /
  29.6. Retrospective, single centre; laser FU 15 vs ~85 months.
- [COHORT] SiLaC 509 pts, Int J Colorectal Dis 2026 (doi 10.1007/s00384-026-05254-z; abstract
  via search, paywalled): median return to work 7 days, median healing 37 days, recurrence 4.4%
  of healed, median FU 29 months.
- [COHORT] World J Surg 2026 (doi 10.1002/wjs.70299; abstract via search, 403 on fetch): final
  recurrence 34.6% primary vs 60.8% recurrent, median 4 years.
- [RCT secondary analysis] Bergus 2024, Ann Surg Open (PMC11415133): effect of laser epilation on
  recurrence may vary by subgroup. Weak support → copy says "pode reduzir".

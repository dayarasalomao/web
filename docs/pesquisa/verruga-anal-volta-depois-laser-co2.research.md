# verruga-anal-volta-depois-laser-co2 — scoped evidence notes

Accessed 2026-11-26. Origin: draft supplied by Dra. Dayara (Nov/2026), checked against sources.
Scope: **recurrence of anogenital warts after CO2 laser** and what reduces it. Not the procedure
itself (`hpv-perianal-laser-co2`), not recognition (`hpv-anal-identificar-tratar`), not
differential of lumps (`lesoes-verrugas-nodulos-regiao-anal-o-que-observar`).
Notes only — not publishable prose.

## Verified

- [GUIDELINE] Recurrence "can occur after any treatment modality"; "limited evidence that any
  specific treatment is associated with a reduced recurrence rate following initial clearance".
  (Source #1, BASHH 2024, "Recurrence")
- [GUIDELINE] Lesions at more than one anatomical site may be more likely to recur. (Source #1)
- [GUIDELINE] Best evidence for adjunct: 4–8 weeks of imiquimod after CO2 laser; "evidence is
  lacking" for other ablation or other topicals. Recommendation graded **2A**. (Source #1)
- [GUIDELINE] Recalcitrant/persistently recurrent warts → prioritise ablation (laser,
  electrosurgery, excision) — **1A**. (Source #1)
- [GUIDELINE] Smoking: higher prevalence, reduced HPV clearance, higher recurrence; cessation
  advice **1B**. Consistent condom use **1B**. (Source #1)
- [GUIDELINE] Atypical features → urgent biopsy (1D); biopsy if non-response (2D); low threshold
  for biopsy in internal warts. (Source #1)
- [GUIDELINE] HPV vaccine: trials failed to show therapeutic benefit for clearance or recurrence;
  not recommended as treatment; offer per national schedule. (Source #1)
- [SR/MA] Ablation + self-applied topical → higher complete clearance at 4–24 weeks than ablation
  alone; 4 and 8 weeks of imiquimod after CO2 similar. Abstract only. (Source #3)
- Imiquimod local reactions (erythema, excoriation, erosion) in 70–80%. (Source #1)

## Changed from the supplied draft — ⚠ VERIFICAR COM A MÉDICA

- **"Retorno em 1 a 3 meses é recomendado"** — BASHH says *routine follow-up is not required*
  once warts resolved. Rewritten as "combine com o seu médico quando reavaliar".
- **"Avaliação dos parceiros sexuais"** — BASHH: *routine evaluation or notification of partners
  is not required*. Rewritten as optional orientation for partners who want it.
- **"~7% de recaída aos 6 meses com laser + imiquimode"** — not found in an opened source. Removed.
- **"0% com poucas lesões × ~50% com >10 lesões (eletrocautério + imiquimode)"** — study not
  located; one cohort (Silvera 2014) found extent was *not* a predictor. Numbers removed; kept the
  qualitative "lesões numerosas exigem mais sessões".
- **"20% a 65% em 3–6 meses"** — not in BASHH; review literature cites ~23–67% at ≤6 months but
  the exact source was not opened. Kept qualitative ("taxas variam bastante").

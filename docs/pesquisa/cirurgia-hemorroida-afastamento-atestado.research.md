# cirurgia-hemorroida-afastamento-atestado — scoped evidence notes

Accessed 2026-11-12. Scope: **time off work (afastamento/atestado) and return to driving,
exercise and heavy effort** after haemorrhoid procedures, by technique. Not a comparison of
pain, recurrence or indication (owned by `pos-operatorio-cirurgia-hemorroidas-o-que-esperar`
and `hemorroidas-qual-tecnica-cirurgica-escolher`), not a day-by-day CO2 timeline (owned by
`primeiros-30-dias-hemorroidectomia-laser-co2`), not banding pain (owned by
`ligadura-elastica-doi-recuperacao-cuidados`).

Demand: Search Console (ago–nov/2026) shows ~300 combined impressions at position 8–10 for
"cirurgia de hemorroida quantos dias de atestado" and variations (`pauta-posts-2026-11.md`, A1).

## 1. Definition

- [EXPERT-OPINION] Excisional haemorrhoidectomy cuts the haemorrhoids out and leaves a wound
  that takes time to heal. (Source #2)
- [EXPERT-OPINION] Banding places an elastic band on the internal component; the tissue falls
  off days later. Outpatient, no anaesthetic required. (Sources #3, #5)
- [SR/MA] Laser haemorrhoidoplasty (diode) is a non-excisional procedure for grade II/III
  internal haemorrhoids. (Sources #6, #7)
- [RCT] CO2 laser can be used as the cutting instrument in a Milligan-Morgan excision; it
  remains an excisional operation. (Source #8)
- "Atestado" (Brazil) ≈ fit note / sick note; in Brazil it is a medical document governed by
  CFM, not a fixed entitlement per procedure. (Source #9)

## 3. Natural history of recovery (what drives time off)

- [EXPERT-OPINION] Return to work depends on how the patient feels and on whether the job is
  physically active. (Sources #1, #2)
- [EXPERT-OPINION] Pain is typically most intense in the first days and improves significantly
  within about two weeks after haemorrhoidectomy. (Source #1)
- [EXPERT-OPINION] Healing after surgery "can be a difficult and painful healing process that
  can last up to 6 weeks"; spotting can persist 6–8 weeks; mucous discharge up to 6 weeks.
  (Source #2) — quoted, do not reuse wording.
- [EXPERT-OPINION] Recovery "can take a few weeks and can be painful". (Source #4)
- [EXPERT-OPINION] "It may be up to 2-4 weeks before one is able to resume your full level of
  activities" after hemorrhoid surgery. (Source #5) — quoted, do not reuse wording.
- [EXPERT-OPINION] Constipation/straining increases pain and bleeding; laxatives and stool
  softeners are routinely given. (Sources #1, #2, #4)

## 4. Red flags (post-procedure; same for every technique)

- [EXPERT-OPINION] Heavy or persistent bleeding, large clots → emergency care. (Sources #2, #3)
- [EXPERT-OPINION] Unable to pass urine → urgent assessment (catheter may be needed; delay can
  damage the bladder). (Sources #3, #5)
- [EXPERT-OPINION] Fever, worsening pain instead of improving, signs of infection → contact the
  team. (Sources #1, #3, #4)

## 7. Procedure and recovery — time off by technique

### Conventional excisional haemorrhoidectomy
- [EXPERT-OPINION] Typically **2 weeks for desk-based jobs, 4 weeks for manual/physical jobs**;
  return "as soon as you feel comfortable". (Source #1, Royal Free London)
- [EXPERT-OPINION] "Most people take about 2 weeks off work, but this depends on how you feel
  and if your job is active." (Source #2, Guy's and St Thomas') — quoted, do not reuse.
- [EXPERT-OPINION] "You may need to take 1 to 2 weeks off work to recover." (Source #4,
  Healthdirect AU) — quoted, do not reuse.
- [EXPERT-OPINION] Full level of activities may take up to 2–4 weeks. (Source #5, ASCRS)
- Conflict to report: AU source says 1–2 weeks; UK trusts say ~2 weeks (desk) to 4 weeks
  (manual). Present as a range that depends on job type, not as one number.
- Cross-check (not cited on page): Milton Keynes University Hospital leaflet gives the same
  2 weeks desk / 4 weeks manual and 1 week without heavy lifting.
  https://www.mkuh.nhs.uk/patient-information-leaflet/haemorrhoidectomy (opened 2026-11-12).

### Diode laser haemorrhoidoplasty ("sem corte")
- [SR/MA] Wee et al. 2023 (Ann Coloproctol; 9 studies, 661 patients, grade II/III; 3 RCTs,
  6 non-randomised): LH returned to work or daily activities earlier than conventional
  haemorrhoidectomy — **MD −11.81 days (95% CI −19.39 to −4.23; 4 studies, n=420)**. Authors ask
  for confirmation in larger well-designed trials. (Source #6; full text read)
- [SR/MA] Pompeu et al. 2026 (Am Surg; 15 RCTs, 1,099 patients): earlier return to daily
  activities, **MD −10.78 days**; certainty of evidence **low to very low**; long-term durability
  uncertain; supports a preference-sensitive choice, not replacement of excision. Brazilian
  authors, English-language peer review. (Source #7; abstract read via PubMed)
- Note: both report a *difference* versus conventional surgery, not an absolute number of days.
  The wide CI (≈4 to 19 days) must be preserved — do not convert to "volta em X dias".
- [EXPERT-OPINION] Oxford University Hospitals leaflet: "After laser haemorrhoidoplasty,
  recovery is usually quicker." (Search-engine excerpt only; PDF returned 403 on direct
  download → **not cited**.)
- [COHORT] Australian pilot (Ann Coloproctol 2024, PMC10915529): median return to work 6 days
  (LH, n=30) vs 13 days (historical hemorrhoidectomy cohort, n=43). Small, historical control.
  Full text read through search tool; direct fetch later returned 403 → **not cited on page**.

### CO2 laser excisional haemorrhoidectomy
- [RCT] Pandini et al. 2006 (Colorectal Dis; University of São Paulo; 40 patients, grade III/IV,
  Milligan-Morgan with CO2 laser vs cold scalpel; patients blinded): **no differences** in
  postoperative pain, complications, healing time, **return to normal activities** or
  satisfaction. Pain at first bowel movement was higher in the laser group. (Source #8;
  abstract read)
- Finding: no comparative evidence located that CO2 laser shortens time off work versus
  conventional excision. Larger CO2 series found are single-centre, non-randomised or in
  low-visibility journals; not used.
- ⚠ Conflict with existing site copy: treatment page and other posts state CO2 gives
  "cicatrização acelerada" and less pain. This post does not repeat or contradict it; it frames
  CO2 time off as following the excisional reference. **⚠ VERIFICAR COM A MÉDICA.**

### Rubber band ligation
- [EXPERT-OPINION] "Rubber band placement is not associated with any significant recovery time
  for most people"; dull ache/pressure 1–3 days. (Source #5) — quoted, do not reuse.
- [EXPERT-OPINION] Usual activities the day after banding; avoid heavy exercise (jogging,
  cycling) for the rest of the day; if sedated, no driving for 24 h. (Source #3)
- [EXPERT-OPINION] St Mark's Hospital banding leaflet: "return to normal activities as soon as
  you feel comfortable"; bleeding 7–10 days after when the scab comes off. (PDF opened
  2026-11-12; dated November 2012 → older than the 7-year recency rule, **not cited on page**.)
  https://www.stmarkshospital.nhs.uk/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/Haemorrhoids-banding.pdf

### Driving
- [EXPERT-OPINION] Do not drive for at least 48 h after surgery (anaesthetic and pain
  medication); then only when confident and comfortable. (Source #1)
- [EXPERT-OPINION] Cannot drive straight after a general anaesthetic. (Source #4)
- [EXPERT-OPINION] Milton Keynes leaflet adds the practical test: able to perform an emergency
  stop safely (cross-check only).
- Brazil note: the "check with your insurer" advice is UK-specific; do not import.

### Exercise and heavy effort
- [EXPERT-OPINION] Avoid heavy lifting or strenuous exercise (fast running, cycling, horse
  riding) for 1 week after haemorrhoidectomy. (Source #1)
- [EXPERT-OPINION] Avoid heavy activity — lifting, exercise, running — "during the first week or
  so"; build up gradually; no swimming until the wound has healed. (Source #2)
- [EXPERT-OPINION] Gentle walking encouraged as soon as possible. (Source #1)
- Note: UK trusts give the *minimum* restriction; return to heavy manual work is the 4-week
  figure above. Do not present "1 week" as clearance for gym/heavy work.

## 9. Patient questions (FAQ) — sketches, not copy

1. How many days of atestado after haemorrhoid surgery? → No fixed number; defined by the
   physician from technique, recovery and job; UK reference ~2 weeks desk / ~4 weeks manual.
   (Sources #1, #9)
2. Does laser mean fewer days off? → Diode LH: on average ~11 days earlier than conventional in
   reviews, low certainty. CO2 excision: one RCT found no difference. (Sources #6, #7, #8)
3. Banding — do I need time off? → Most resume usual activity next day. (Sources #3, #5)
4. When can I drive? → Not for ≥48 h after anaesthesia; then when pain allows safe reaction.
   (Sources #1, #4)
5. When can I go back to the gym / lift weights? → Not in the first week; heavy work longer.
   (Sources #1, #2)
6. Can the atestado be extended? → Yes, it reflects the recovery assessed at the visit; return
   for review if recovery is slower. (Source #9; Royal Free notes GP can extend if more time is
   needed — UK system)
7. Can I work from home earlier? → Depends on pain and sitting tolerance; not addressed by
   sources — do not answer with a number.

## 10. Scope boundary

- Brazilian labour/social-security rules (employer days, INSS perícia) are outside medical
  scope and were not researched: **do not state any day threshold**. Suggest the patient
  confirm with HR / company occupational physician.
- Outpatient practice in Campo Grande/MS; complications needing emergency care go to an
  emergency service. Do not imply in-house emergency infrastructure.

## 13. Do not state

- Any fixed number of atestado days, or "a Dra. dá X dias".
- "Laser = volta ao trabalho em X dias" as an absolute; diode evidence is a mean *difference*
  with low/very low certainty and a wide CI.
- That CO2 laser shortens time off versus conventional excision (no RCT support found).
- "Sem dor", "recuperação imediata", "no dia seguinte já está trabalhando" for surgery.
- Brazilian labour-law thresholds (15 days, INSS) — not researched.
- UK self-certification (7 days) and insurer rules — not applicable in Brazil.
- That "1 week without heavy effort" means clearance for heavy manual work.

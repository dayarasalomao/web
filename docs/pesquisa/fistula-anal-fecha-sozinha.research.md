# fistula-anal-fecha-sozinha — scoped evidence notes

Accessed 2026-11-26. Origin: draft supplied by Dra. Dayara (Nov/2026), checked against sources.
Scope: **does an anal fistula heal on its own** and when FiLaC fits. Not formation
(`por-que-a-fistula-anal-aparece`), not abscess drainage (`drenagem-abscesso-perianal-e-risco-de-fistula`),
not VAAFT+FiLaC procedure (`fistula-anal-laser-vaaft-filac`). Notes only.

## Verified (abstract level)

- [SR/MA] Elfeki 2020, Tech Coloproctol (PMID 32065306): 7 studies, weighted primary healing
  ~67.3%, median FU 23.7 months; continence affection ~1% (minor soiling); calls for RCTs.
- [SR/MA] Int J Colorectal Dis 2025 (doi 10.1007/s00384-025-04995-7): recurrence ~18.5%,
  reoperation ~36%, new-onset incontinence ~0.6%. Exact title not confirmed. ⚠
- [COHORT] Wilhelm 2015 (PMID 25724967): long-term FiLaC results.

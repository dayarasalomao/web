## 1. Definition
- [EXPERT-OPINION] Hemorrhoidal tissue is normal anatomy; disease describes symptomatic enlargement/prolapse. (Source #1)

## 2. Who it affects
- No prevalence statistic required or retained for this scope.

## 3. Symptoms and natural history
- [EXPERT-OPINION] Symptoms may be intermittent and improve with nonsurgical measures; persistent prolapse/recurrent symptoms warrant reassessment. (Source #1)

## 4. Red flags
- [EXPERT-OPINION] Bleeding needs appropriate assessment; improvement with topical treatment does not rule out other diagnoses. (Source #1)
- No age-based cancer screening threshold is added; recurrent bleeding needs individualized evaluation.

## 5. Diagnosis
- [EXPERT-OPINION] History and physical assessment guide diagnosis. Images and treatment response do not replace examination. (Source #1)

## 6. Conservative vs procedural management
- [EXPERT-OPINION] Dietary/bowel measures, office treatments and surgery have different indications. (Sources #1, #2)

## 7. Procedure and recovery
- Use only the supplied clinical scope and verified general recovery facts. No device-specific recovery estimate is inferred from unrelated surgery.

## 8. Outcomes
- No lifetime cure promise or absolute irreversible-dilation statement; no recurrence rate without study population/follow-up. ⚠ VERIFICAR COM A MÉDICA.

## 9. Patient questions
- Crisis settles? It can. Recurrence? Possible, varies by disease and treatment. Everyone needs surgery? No. Cancer transformation? Avoid conflating hemorrhoids with other causes of bleeding. (Source #1)

## 10. Scope boundary
- Outpatient patient education in Campo Grande/MS. Acute complications need appropriate urgent care; do not imply emergency infrastructure or in-house colonoscopy.

## 11. Terminology map
- Hemorrhoidectomy → hemorroidectomia → cirurgia com retirada de tecido.
- Hemorrhoidoplasty → hemorroidoplastia → laser de diodo, “sem corte”.
- Thrombosed external hemorrhoid → hemorroida externa trombosada → caroço duro e dolorido.

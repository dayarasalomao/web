# hemorroida-nao-melhora-quando-operar — scoped evidence notes

Accessed 2026-11-26. Origin: draft supplied by Dra. Dayara (Nov/2026), checked against sources.
Scope: **when persistent haemorrhoid symptoms justify surgery** and what laser haemorrhoidoplasty
offers. Not technique-by-technique choice (`hemorroidas-qual-tecnica-cirurgica-escolher`), not
diode-laser procedure page (`hemorroidas-sem-corte-laser-diodo`), not time off
(`cirurgia-hemorroida-afastamento-atestado`). Notes only.

## Verified (Wee 2023, Ann Coloproctol 39(1):3–10 — full text read)

- 9 studies, 661 pts (3 RCT, 4 prospective, 2 retrospective), grade II/III, comparator
  Milligan-Morgan. No GRADE; authors ask for larger well-designed trials.
- Operative time MD −12.65 min; blood loss MD −19.78 mL; POD1 VAS −2.09, POD7 −3.94; analgesic
  use RR 0.59; return to work/activities MD −11.81 days; postoperative bleeding RR 0.18.
- Urinary outcome RR 0.30 (5 studies) — text says "incontinence", figure caption says
  "retention": ambiguous. ⚠
- Recurrence at 12 months (2 RCTs): no significant difference.

## Not fully read

- Asian J Surg 2024 (PMID 38762410): abstract via search snippet — 17 RCTs, 1,196 pts; anal
  stenosis OR 0.14; urinary retention *not* significantly different. ⚠ conflicting with other

- Pompeu et al. (PMID 42503488): low/very-low certainty, as in the afastamento post.
